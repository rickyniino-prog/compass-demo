COMPASS — Public Demo

This edition is for demonstrations with fictional information only. There is no secure login, cloud database, cross-device synchronization, or official approval workflow. The account dropdown previews roles; it does not authenticate anyone.

Training events, Long-Range Training Calendar, tasks, document drafts, and individual Troops-to-Task assignments are linked. Changes are saved only in this browser's local storage; clearing browser data or opening on another device will not carry them over.

Do not enter real Soldier names, private records, nonpublic locations or schedules, medical status, CUI, classified or operationally sensitive information.

Use the bottom navigation on phones. Swipe horizontally inside large tables; tap events, tasks, and matrix cells to explore fictional records.
